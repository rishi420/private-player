How to watch videos from your computer on Apple TV

Family videos, recorded lectures, downloaded courses: they sit on your computer, but you want them on the big screen.

Updated November 2026

1. AirPlay from a Mac (free)

Play the video on your Mac and choose your Apple TV from the AirPlay menu. Easy, but your Mac has to stay on and nearby, and the video plays only while it streams.

2. Home Sharing (free)

Turn on Home Sharing on your computer and on Apple TV, using the same Apple Account, then open the Computers app on Apple TV. It shows videos from your computer's library. The computer must be on, and files need to be in formats Apple supports.

3. Copy the files to Apple TV: Private Player

  1. Install Private Player from the App Store on your Apple TV.
  2. Import your videos from a web browser or from your Mac or PC.
  3. Watch them on Apple TV, including .mkv, .avi and .wmv files, without keeping your computer on.
See Private Player on the App Store Apple TV app. Free to download; the optional Pro subscription unlocks unlimited watch time.
