Play your own videos on Apple TV, behind a passcode

Private Player is an Apple TV app. It plays the video, music, photo and text files you bring to it, including formats Apple TV doesn't play on its own, and keeps them behind a passcode.

See it on the App Store For Apple TV: search "Private Player" in the App Store on your Apple TV. Free to download; the optional Pro subscription unlocks unlimited watch time.

What you can do

  • Play many formats: videos in .mkv, .avi, .mp4, .3gp, .mov, .webm, .wmv and more; music in .flac, .mp3, .ogg, .wav, .wma and more; photos; and text files like .txt, .rtf and .html.
  • Lock it: set a secret passcode so others using the TV can't open your files.
  • Bring files in: import from a web browser, from your Mac or PC, or on the device.
  • Everything on the big screen: movies, music, photos and text, in one place.
